---------- Title: IEEE-754 floating-point converter LastUpdated: Saturday, 07 November 2026 ---------- # IEEE-754 floating-point converter I made this tool to convert floating-point numbers between decimal and their underlying hex representation. There was already a tool for that [here](https://www.h-schmidt.net/FloatConverter/IEEE754.html), but that only works for single-precision (32 bit) floats, and I needed double-precision (64 bit) floats instead. It's a simple enough program so I went ahead and just made it... well over a year ago.
